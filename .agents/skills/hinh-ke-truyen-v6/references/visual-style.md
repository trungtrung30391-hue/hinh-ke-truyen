# Visual Style — Hình Kể Truyện V6

Default:
**Cinematic living-history collage, archival documentary aesthetic**

Đặc trưng:
- aged handmade paper
- yellowed newspapers
- antique engravings
- torn paper edges
- glue marks
- subtle halftone
- muted historical palette
- cinematic documentary lighting
- realistic anatomy
- period-accurate props/architecture

## Visual Lock preset

- Standard/YouTube aspect ratio: 16:9
- Shorts/TikTok: 9:16
- Style: cinematic living-history collage
- Palette: warm sand, sepia, dark brown, muted gold
- Lighting: dramatic documentary lighting
- Lens feel: 35mm cinematic
- Texture: aged paper, archival print, subtle halftone
- Composition: single-scene, one moment only
- Restrictions: no storyboard, no grid, no split screen, no multi-panel layout, no text, no logo, no watermark

Luôn thêm:
`single-scene cinematic composition, one moment only, one image, no storyboard, no grid, no split screen, no multi-panel layout`

Negative constraints gợi ý:
`modern objects, anachronistic clothing, futuristic technology, inconsistent face, duplicate people, deformed hands, wrong architecture style, random text, logo, watermark, low detail`

"Collage" chỉ là chất liệu/thẩm mỹ, không có nghĩa là ghép nhiều cảnh trong một ảnh.
